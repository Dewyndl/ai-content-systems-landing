What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the name, work email, message, consent state, page path, and basic delivery metadata needed to reply. We do not collect payment details or sensitive personal information through this site.

Why we use it

We use enquiry details only to respond to your request and assess whether our services are a fit. We do not sell your information.

Third parties

Production enquiries are sent to the configured lead webhook or CRM selected by Signal Systems. Providers are granted only the data needed to handle the enquiry and are responsible for their own security and retention terms.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold, request correction, or ask us to delete an enquiry, email the address used in your project correspondence. This notice should be reviewed and completed with the responsible legal entity and contact address before launch.
